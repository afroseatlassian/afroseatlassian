## About me

- 🔧 Deep hands-on with **Jira/JSM administration, ITSM, workflows, Jira Automation, Assets/CMDB, permissions, schemes and custom fields**

- 📊 Built and maintain **20+ active Jira, Power BI and eazyBI dashboards** for PMO, QA, SLA, defect and management reporting

- 🔌 Integrated Jira with **GitHub, Jenkins, Bitbucket, Bamboo and ServiceNow** for CI/CD visibility and bi-directional ITSM sync
